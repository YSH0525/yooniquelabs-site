Crane Dreams Privacy Policy

한국어 · English · 日本語

Yoonique Labs (Korean business registration no. 770-11-03155, "we") processes the personal information of users of the mobile game "Crane Dreams" (com.yooniquelabs.cranedreams, the "App") as described below, in compliance with applicable laws including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable US state privacy laws.

Effective date: October 3, 2026 · Revised: October 4, 2026 (Google Play Games Services added for cloud save and usage statistics; English and Japanese versions added, US state privacy choices added)

1. Information we collect directly

The App has no sign-up or login, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number or email address on our servers. Your coins, doll collection, settings and purchase records (transaction IDs) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Your advertising choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads to turn off personalized ads.
  • In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent screen is shown when you first launch the App. You can change your choice at any time under "Ad Privacy Options" in the App's settings.
  • In US states with privacy laws, such as California, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information) under "Ad Privacy Options" in the App's settings.
  • If you purchase "Remove Ads", banner and full-screen ads are no longer shown. Reward ads that you choose to watch remain available.

4. Retention

We do not store personal information ourselves. If you contact us by email, your message and email address are kept for 3 years after the inquiry is resolved and then deleted.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA, or under 14 in Korea). Minors must obtain a parent's or guardian's permission before making in-app purchases, and parents can manage purchases with Google Play's purchase authentication settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Game records stored on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App or via Android Settings > Apps > Crane Dreams > Storage > Clear data. For any request, please contact us at the email address below. We do not sell personal information for money.

7. International transfers

The third-party services above may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. These providers use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required by law.

8. Odds information

Grab success rates and doll appearance rates are available on the odds page and under "Odds" in the App.
