Crane Dreams Odds

한국어 · English · 日本語

App: Crane Dreams (com.yooniquelabs.cranedreams) · Applies to version 1.0.1 and later · Provided by Yoonique Labs

Each play costs 1 coin. Coins can be earned through free refills, ad rewards, or purchases. Dolls you win are virtual items recorded only in the in-game collection and cannot be exchanged for cash or physical goods. If the rates change, we will announce it in advance on this page and in the game.

1. Grab success rate

Base success rate by doll difficulty when the claw grabs the exact center of the doll.

DifficultyBase success rate
★ (1)90%
★★ (2)78%
★★★ (3)62%
★★★★ (4)46%
★★★★★ (5)32%
  • Actual success rate = base rate × (0.3 + 0.7 × center alignment) + losing-streak bonus. Center alignment ranges from 1 (exact center) to 0 (27 cm or more off), based on the distance between the claw center and the doll center.
  • If 3 or more dolls overlap under the claw, ×0.9 is applied. The maximum is 97%.
  • After a successful grab, the chance of the doll slipping while lifting is (100% − success rate) × 55%, limited to between 3% and 50%. It is 0% for a guaranteed win (pity).

2. Losing-streak bonus (pity)

  • Each miss raises the success rate by 7%p.
  • After 8 misses in a row, the next try is a guaranteed win with no slipping if the claw grabs near the doll's center (alignment above 0.15).
  • The bonus resets to 0 when you win a doll.
