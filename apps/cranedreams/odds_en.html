Crane Dreams Odds

한국어 · English · 日本語

App: Crane Dreams (com.yooniquelabs.cranedreams) · Applies to version 1.1.1 and later · Provided by Yoonique Labs

Each play costs 1 coin. Coins can be earned through free refills, ad rewards, or purchases. Dolls you win are virtual items recorded only in the in-game collection and cannot be exchanged for cash or physical goods. If the rates change, we will announce it in advance on this page and in the game.

1. Grab success rate

Base success rate by doll difficulty when the claw grabs the exact center of the doll.

DifficultyBase success rate
★ (1)90%
★★ (2)78%
★★★ (3)62%
★★★★ (4)46%
★★★★★ (5)32%
  • Actual success rate = base rate × (0.3 + 0.7 × center alignment) + losing-streak bonus. Center alignment ranges from 1 (exact center) to 0 (27 cm or more off), based on the distance between the claw center and the doll center.
  • If 3 or more dolls overlap under the claw, ×0.9 is applied. The maximum is 97%.
  • After a successful grab, the chance of the doll dropping while lifting or being carried to the chute is (100% − success rate) × 55%, limited to between 3% and 50%. When it drops, 45% of the time it happens at the top and 55% of the time while being carried to the chute. It is 0% for a guaranteed win (pity).

2. Losing-streak bonus (pity)

  • Each miss raises the success rate by 7%p.
  • After 8 misses in a row, the next try is a guaranteed win with no slipping if the claw grabs near the doll's center (alignment above 0.15).
  • The bonus resets to 0 when you win a doll.

3. Doll appearance rates

Each machine has its own set of dolls. When you win a doll, one new doll from that machine fills the empty spot at the rates in section 3-2 below. The 'Golden Crown Bear' appears in every machine, and one is always included when the 'Forest Friends' machine is first filled.

New doll series unlock as the number of different dolls in your collection grows. Only dolls from unlocked series appear in the machine. Dolls from locked series do not appear and cannot be exchanged for star shards.

4. Star Shard exchange

  • Winning a doll you already own gives Star Shards: Common 1, Rare 2, Legendary 5.
  • You can pick any doll you have not won yet in the collection and exchange Star Shards for it: Common 10, Rare 20, Legendary 40. Exchanges involve no randomness; you receive exactly the doll you picked.
  • Star Shards are not sold and cannot be exchanged for cash or coins.
